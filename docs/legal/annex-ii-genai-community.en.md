This Annex forms part of the Privacy Policy of My Tech Plan, S.L. and governs the processing of personal data of the members, participants, subscribers and collaborators of **GenAI Community EU** (the "Community"), as well as of people who visit or use the website **genaicommunity.eu**.

GenAI Community EU is an initiative of My Tech Plan, S.L. whose purpose is to connect professionals working in artificial intelligence, encourage knowledge sharing and create opportunities for networking, learning and collaboration within the European technology ecosystem.

The Community carries out its activities through digital platforms, in-person and online meetups, local chapters, newsletters and specialised publications, and through its website genaicommunity.eu.

## 2. Personal data processed

Depending on how each user takes part and the information they provide, the following categories of data may be processed:

**Identification and contact data:**
- First and last name.
- Email address.
- City and country of residence or professional activity.

**Professional data:**
- Company or organisation.
- Job title or professional role.
- Sector or field of activity.
- Years of professional experience and experience with generative AI, as stated by the user in the Community membership form or when completing their Slack profile.
- LinkedIn or other professional profiles provided voluntarily.

**Membership application data** (form at genaicommunity.eu/join):
- Personal introduction text.
- Reasons for joining the Community and the ways they want to take part.
- Acceptance of the Code of Conduct.
- Preferences and consents given in the form (newsletter, communications about other My Tech Plan projects, introduction in Slack).
- Date and time of the application.

**Community participation data:**
- Slack username and profile photo.
- Local chapter the person belongs to or whose activities they take part in.
- Participation in Community channels, conversations and activities.
- Information voluntarily shared in Community spaces.

**Event registration and attendance data:**
- Eventbrite registration information.
- Registration or ticket identifiers.
- Attendance or check-in status.
- History of participation in Community meetups and activities.

**Subscription and communication data:**
- Subscription status and preferences.
- Consents given.
- Interactions with newsletters and digital publications, including opens and clicks where applicable.
- Unsubscribe requests and objections to communications.

**Contact form data** (general contact, each chapter's contact form and sponsorship): name, email, company where given, reason for the enquiry and message content.

**Data of Chapter Leads published on the website:** name, photo, job title, company, biography, quote and professional links (LinkedIn, GitHub or personal website) provided by the Chapter Lead.

**Technical browsing data:** IP address and browser and device data, processed by the technical services described in section 4.4 to deliver the website, protect it against abuse and, with consent, measure its use.

As a general rule, My Tech Plan does not request special categories of personal data in order to take part in the Community.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

### 3.1 Managing the Community

**Purpose:** managing the onboarding of members, administering access to digital spaces, facilitating professional communication and collaboration, coordinating chapters and carrying out the activities of GenAI Community EU.

When the membership form is completed, My Tech Plan sends the applicant an email with the link to join the Community's Slack workspace.

**Legal basis:** performance of the participation relationship in the Community, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### 3.2 Organising and promoting events

**Purpose:** managing registrations, attendance, confirmations, logistics, agenda changes and the other communications needed to organise Community meetups.

Communications about GenAI Community EU activities related to members' participation may also be sent, distinguishing operational communications from promotional ones that require consent or another legal basis.

**Legal basis:** performance of the relationship arising from registration or participation and, where applicable, the data subject's consent.

### 3.3 Professional profiles and statistics

**Purpose:** analysing the professional make-up of the Community, including sectors, roles, geographic location and years of professional experience.

This data may be used to produce internal statistics, analyse how the Community evolves and tailor activities, content and networking opportunities to its members' professional profiles.

**Legal basis:** My Tech Plan's legitimate interest in understanding and improving how the Community works, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, subject to a prior assessment and respecting the data subject's right to object.

Wherever possible, results are presented in aggregated or anonymised form.

### 3.4 Newsletter and digital publications

**Purpose:** managing subscriptions and sending content about artificial intelligence, innovation, technology, GenAI Community EU activities and news from its professional ecosystem.

**Legal basis:** the data subject's consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Subscribing is voluntary and can be cancelled at any time through the unsubscribe options included in the communications.

### 3.5 Communications about other My Tech Plan projects

My Tech Plan may offer members the option of receiving communications about other company initiatives, including:

- GenAI Summit EU.
- GenAI Workshop Fest.
- Other events, programmes, services and professional activities organised by My Tech Plan.

These communications may include promotional information, news, participation opportunities and commercial offers.

**Legal basis:** the data subject's specific, freely given, informed and unambiguous consent, given through a dedicated checkbox, unticked by default, in the membership form.

Joining GenAI Community EU does not automatically mean accepting commercial communications about other projects.

This consent is independent of the processing needed for the ordinary management of the Community and of any authorisation to share data with sponsors or partners.

### 3.6 Sharing data with sponsors and partners

At certain GenAI Community EU events, participants may voluntarily authorise the sharing of certain personal and professional data with sponsors or partners linked to the event.

The purpose is to let those organisations contact the data subjects directly to share professional information, products, services, collaboration or talent opportunities, or commercial communications related to technology and artificial intelligence.

**Legal basis:** the data subject's prior, specific, informed and unambiguous consent.

Not giving this consent does not prevent anyone from being part of the Community, registering for its activities or attending its meetups.

### 3.7 Introducing new members in Slack

**Purpose:** welcoming each new member and introducing them to the rest of the Community. When someone joins the Slack workspace, an automated Community assistant looks up their membership application by email address and posts their name, job title, company, city, introduction text and LinkedIn profile in the #new-members channel. This post is visible to all members of the Slack workspace.

**Legal basis:** the legitimate interest of My Tech Plan and of the members in getting to know each other and facilitating networking, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The membership form explains this post before it is submitted and includes a checkbox to opt out of being introduced; anyone who ticks it receives a welcome without their details. It is also possible to object at any time by writing to rgpd@mytechplan.com, in which case the post will be removed.

This introduction does not make you visible in the GenAI-ID community directory, which is only switched on with your consent in "My account".

If there is no membership application matching the email address someone joins Slack with, the assistant sends them a direct message inviting them to complete the form and alerts the workspace administrators, giving them that email address. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in ensuring people join through the Community's procedure.

### 3.8 Public profiles of Chapter Leads

**Purpose:** publicly introducing the leads of each local chapter on the Community website, with their name, photo, job title, company, biography, quote and professional links. This data is also included in the page code as structured data, so that search engines and other services can correctly identify the Chapter Leads.

**Legal basis:** the consent of the Chapter Lead, who provides the information and approves its publication. It can be withdrawn at any time by writing to rgpd@mytechplan.com, in which case the profile will be removed from the website.

### 3.9 Enquiries received through the website

**Purpose:** handling enquiries sent through the general contact, chapter contact and sponsorship forms, and replying to the sender.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest in handling the enquiries received and, where the enquiry concerns a possible sponsorship or collaboration agreement, taking pre-contractual steps at the data subject's request.

### 3.10 Website security and abuse prevention

**Purpose:** protecting the website's forms against automated submissions and abuse with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, which analyses interaction with the page.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

### 3.11 Measuring website use

**Purpose:** understanding, in aggregate, how the website is used in order to improve it.

- **Vercel Web Analytics:** aggregated visit statistics, without cookies and without identifying the person. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used.
- **Google Analytics 4**, through Google Tag Manager: only activated if the visitor accepts it in the cookie banner. **Legal basis:** consent. ⚠️ To confirm: described because it is due to be activated; until then this item must be removed or say it is not in use.

## 4. Technology platforms used

### 4.1 Slack — Community platform

Slack is the main digital space for interaction and communication in GenAI Community EU.

Through this platform the following may be managed:

- Members joining and leaving.
- Personal and professional profiles.
- Information about years of professional experience.
- Participation in topic channels and local chapters.
- Community communications.
- Posts, conversations and shared files.
- Coordination of professional activities and meetups.
- The automatic introduction of new members described in section 3.7.

Profile data and information posted in shared channels may be visible to other Community members, according to the settings and permissions configured in Slack.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive data or third parties' personal information without the relevant authorisation.

### 4.2 Eventbrite — Event registration and management

Eventbrite is the central platform for registering for and managing GenAI Community EU in-person and online meetups.

Through this platform the following may be processed:

- Identification and professional data of registrants.
- Information about the event and local chapter.
- Registration confirmations and status.
- Attendance or check-in data.
- Operational communications about the activity.
- Consents given during registration.

Registering for an event does not automatically mean subscribing to newsletters or to commercial communications about other projects, nor authorising the sharing of data with sponsors or partners.

The Community website shows the events published on Eventbrite, with their images. To display them, the visitor's browser downloads them directly from Eventbrite's servers, which receive the visitor's IP address under Eventbrite's own privacy policy. The website sends Eventbrite no other data about the visitor.

### 4.3 beehiiv — Newsletter and blog

beehiiv is the platform used to manage subscriptions, distribute newsletters, publish digital content and manage subscribers' communication preferences.

Identification and contact data, subscription information and interactions with published content may be processed.

Subscribing to the newsletter is independent of membership of the Slack workspace and of registering for events through Eventbrite.

Users can unsubscribe through the links included in the communications.

Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not necessarily mean leaving the Community or cancelling registrations made on other platforms.

The website and its forms use the following services:

| Resend | Sending the website's emails (form notifications and the welcome email) | Ireland (European Union) |

## 5. Centralised data management

Personal data associated with GenAI Community EU is managed under the responsibility of My Tech Plan, S.L.

The Community is managed mainly through the following platforms:

- **Slack:** management of members, profiles and community communications.
- **Eventbrite:** management of registrations, attendees and event communications.
- **beehiiv:** management of subscriptions, newsletters and digital publications.
- **Google Workspace:** record of membership applications received on the website.
- **GenAI-ID:** the shared account of the My Tech Plan ecosystem (id.genaicommunity.eu), where Community membership is recorded as a relationship with the Community; on its own it does not switch on the newsletter, the directory or communications about other projects.

Community membership is recorded in GenAI-ID to keep a single accurate record of each person across the My Tech Plan ecosystem. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in keeping a single accurate record. This merge creates no new subscriptions or permissions: previous preferences are kept and, where unknown, remain "undecided" until the data subject sets them. Processing in GenAI-ID is governed by its own privacy notice.

My Tech Plan may link the records held on these platforms where necessary to:

- Keep members' information up to date.
- Avoid duplicates.
- Manage participation in local chapters.
- Coordinate event registrations and attendance.
- Produce internal statistics.
- Analyse how the Community evolves.
- Manage the data subjects' communication preferences and consents.

Centralising the data does not mean that every platform can automatically use the information collected by the others.

Each processing operation is limited to the purposes stated and the corresponding legal basis.

## 6. Access to data by Chapter Leads and local organisers

GenAI Community EU carries out part of its activity through local chapters, run by Chapter Leads and authorised collaborators.

Chapter Leads may access, with My Tech Plan's authorisation, the data of members and registrants relating to the activities and local chapters assigned to them.

This access is solely for:

- Organising and managing the chapter's events.
- Promoting their chapter's activities and meetups among the recipients they may lawfully address.
- Sending communications about the events, including invitations, confirmations, reminders and changes.
- Coordinating participation and attendance.
- Facilitating networking and interaction between members.
- Following up on activities and assessing participation.

**This data and the related communications are managed centrally through Eventbrite and Slack**, using the access and permissions provided by My Tech Plan.

Enquiries sent through the contact form on a chapter's page go to that chapter's mailbox where one exists, managed by My Tech Plan, and otherwise to the Community's general mailbox.

Chapter Leads are not authorised to:

- Use the data for personal projects or commercial activities unrelated to GenAI Community EU.
- Market, sell or transfer the databases.
- Share information with sponsors, partners or other organisations without authorisation and an applicable legal basis.
- Add the data to personal databases or marketing tools.
- Send communications outside the authorised purposes.
- Access information about other chapters where this is not necessary for their role.

My Tech Plan sets criteria for restricted access, confidentiality and data minimisation, limiting each collaborator's permissions to their responsibilities.

Chapter Leads act within the organisational structure of GenAI Community EU, in accordance with My Tech Plan's instructions and authorisations.

Access to participants' data does not give anyone any rights over the Community's databases.

## 7. Sharing data with sponsors and partners

### 7.1 General conditions

Sharing personal data with sponsors and partners of GenAI Community EU events is optional and requires the data subject's specific consent.

Sponsoring an activity does not give general access to the Slack member base or to beehiiv subscribers.

Sharing is limited to participants who have given the relevant consent for the event and to the recipients they were informed about.

### 7.2 Data that may be shared

Provided they were supplied by the data subject and are covered by the consent given, the following may be shared:

- First and last name.
- Email address.
- Company or organisation.
- Job title.
- Years of professional experience.
- LinkedIn profile, if provided.

Private Slack messages, community conversations, credentials, technical data and any information unrelated to the authorised purpose are never shared.

### 7.3 Sponsors and partners that may receive data

Only organisations authorised by My Tech Plan, identified to the data subject before their consent is requested, and whose collaboration agreement expressly provides for this processing, may receive data.

The recipients and the information needed about the processing will be available in the registration process or through links accessible from it.

Adding new recipients not covered by the initial consent requires informing the data subject and, where applicable, obtaining new consent.

### 7.4 Conditions for sharing

My Tech Plan will only include in lists intended for sponsors and partners people who have given a valid and verifiable authorisation.

Where the sponsorship benefit is linked specifically to actual attendees, the corresponding attendance record will also be checked.

Data may be provided to authorised organisations through structured files or secure tools for managing professional contacts.

Sponsors and partners that receive data generally act as independent controllers for their subsequent processing, and must respect the stated purposes, the applicable law and the data subjects' rights.

My Tech Plan sets out the conditions for this access contractually, including confidentiality, security, purpose limitation and a ban on transfers to unauthorised third parties.

### 7.5 Retention by sponsors and partners

Sponsors and partners may process the data received for a maximum of 12 months from receipt, unless it must be deleted earlier or another valid legal basis justifies a different retention period.

Withdrawing consent prevents My Tech Plan from sharing the data again and triggers the legally required actions regarding the recipient organisations.

## 8. Recipients of the data

Personal data may be accessible, to the extent necessary, to:

- Authorised My Tech Plan staff.
- Authorised Chapter Leads and local organisers.
- Members of the Slack workspace, regarding the introduction described in section 3.7 and the information each person posts in shared channels.
- Any visitor to the website, regarding the public Chapter Lead profiles described in section 3.8.
- The technology providers needed to deliver the services: Slack, Eventbrite, beehiiv, Vercel, Google (Workspace, reCAPTCHA and, if activated, Analytics), Resend and the GenAI-ID infrastructure described in its privacy notice.
- Sponsors and partners expressly authorised by the data subject, only under the conditions in section 7.
- Public authorities and bodies where there is a legal obligation.

Technology providers may act as processors or as independent controllers for certain operations, depending on the nature of the service and their contractual terms. The OpenStreetMap Foundation acts as an independent controller as described in section 4.4.

We do not sell the data or share it with third parties for advertising.

## 9. International transfers

Some providers process or may access the data from outside the European Economic Area to provide the service, in particular Slack, beehiiv, Eventbrite, Google, Vercel and Resend. In those cases we apply an adequacy decision, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request a copy of these safeguards at rgpd@mytechplan.com.

## 10. Data retention

Data is kept for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes described.

**Community members and membership applications:** while the participation relationship remains active and, afterwards, for as long as needed to deal with liabilities, claims or legal obligations.

**Event records:** while each activity is organised and held and, afterwards, according to the criteria in My Tech Plan's general Privacy Policy, which provides for up to 24 months for support, incidents and claims, unless a longer legal period applies.

**Newsletter subscriptions:** while a valid subscription exists, until the data subject unsubscribes or withdraws consent.

**Consents:** up to five years after the relationship ends, to prove they were obtained and to deal with possible legal liabilities.

**Messages and content in Slack:** according to the retention and deletion periods, settings and features applicable to the Community workspace.

**Enquiries received through the contact forms:** ⚠️ Period to be confirmed (for example, the time needed to handle the enquiry and then up to 24 months, in line with event records).

**Public Chapter Lead profiles:** while the person serves as Chapter Lead or until they withdraw consent.

**Data shared with sponsors and partners:** according to the period and conditions set out in section 7.

Where applicable, data will be deleted, anonymised or blocked in accordance with the applicable law.

## 11. Communications and privacy preferences

GenAI Community EU members may take part in different activities and services independently.

Being a member of the Slack workspace, registering on Eventbrite and subscribing through beehiiv do not automatically mean accepting the other types of communication.

My Tech Plan distinguishes between:

- Communications needed to manage participation in the Community and its activities.
- Voluntary subscriptions to newsletters and publications.
- Commercial communications about other My Tech Plan projects.
- Authorisations to share data with sponsors and partners.

The corresponding consents are independent, optional, unticked by default and can be withdrawn where applicable.

My Tech Plan keeps records that make it possible to prove the consents given.

## 12. Data subjects' rights

For any request (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability or withdrawal of consent) write to rgpd@mytechplan.com; we reply within one month. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

If you have a GenAI-ID account, you can also correct your profile, manage your preferences, download your data and delete your account from "My account".

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Where data has been lawfully shared with third parties acting as independent controllers, the obligations of each controller apply.

## 13. Images and audiovisual content

Photos and audiovisual recordings may be taken at GenAI Community EU in-person or online meetups to document and communicate the Community's activities.

The criteria in My Tech Plan's general Privacy Policy apply, prioritising wide shots and providing specific information when a person is the subject of an interview, testimonial or individual audiovisual content.

Photos of Chapter Leads published on the website are governed by section 3.8.

Data subjects can contact rgpd@mytechplan.com to exercise their rights regarding their image.

## 14. Relationship with the general Privacy Policy

This Annex complements the general Privacy Policy of My Tech Plan, S.L.

For anything not expressly covered here, its general provisions on security, rights, retention, cookies, data protection and policy changes apply.

Membership of GenAI Community EU does not imply automatic acceptance of the optional processing provided for GenAI Summit EU, GenAI Workshop Fest or other My Tech Plan projects.

## 15. Consent in forms and registrations

Privacy information must be available in the processes for joining the Community, registering for events and subscribing to communications.

Optional consents must be requested through separate statements that are sufficiently informed and make it possible to prove the authorisation given.

In particular, the following are distinguished:

1. Information about the processing needed to manage participation in the Community and events, provided next to the submit button of each form, which does not require acceptance.
2. Consent to subscribe to the newsletter.
3. Consent to receive commercial communications about other My Tech Plan projects.
4. Specific consent to share data with the sponsors and partners of an event.

In addition, the membership form explains the Slack introduction described in section 3.7 and includes a checkbox to opt out of it.

Not giving consent for the optional purposes does not prevent the data subject from joining the Community or taking part in its activities.

## 16. Cookies and similar technologies on genaicommunity.eu

- **Cookie preferences:** the choice made in the cookie banner is stored in the browser's local storage.
- **Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise:** may use cookies and similar technologies needed to protect the forms (section 3.10).
- **Vercel Web Analytics:** does not use cookies.
- **Google Analytics 4 / Google Tag Manager:** only sets cookies if the visitor accepts them in the banner. ⚠️ See section 3.11.
- **OpenStreetMap and Eventbrite:** set no advertising or tracking cookies from the website; they receive the visitor's IP address as described in sections 4.2 and 4.4.

Visitors can change their choice at any time through the "Manage cookies" link in the footer.

## 17. Minors and automated decisions

GenAI Community EU is not directed at people under 14.

We make no automated decisions with legal effects and carry out no profiling for that purpose.
